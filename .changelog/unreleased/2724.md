[BREAKING][removal][lib,rpc] Removed `NodeRpcClient::has_genesis_commitment`. `Client::ensure_genesis_in_place` checks the store for the genesis block header and its protocol configuration instead ([#2724](https://github.com/0xMiden/rust-sdk/pull/2724)).
